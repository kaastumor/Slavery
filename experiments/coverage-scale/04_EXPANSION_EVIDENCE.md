## Supported cells

- **COV2:1300:E Pandya Empire:** Specialist synthesis supports slavery and dependency in medieval Tamil South India, including likely extensive enslaved agricultural labour from the thirteenth century onward; this is temporally and regionally relevant to the Pandya-period target.
- **COV2:500:F Goguryeo:** Korean historical synthesis identifies nobi as the lowest status group in Goguryeo, with war captivity, punishment/family liability and debt among pathways into the status.
- **COV2:1300:C Mamluk Sultanate:** Slavery was structurally embedded in the Mamluk Sultanate around 1300: military slaves formed the ruling military-political elite while domestic and labour slavery also existed in Egypt and Syria.
- **COV2:1800:C Merina Kingdom:** Slavery and slave export were materially present in the Merina/Imerina political economy around 1800, but specialist scholarship rejects treating slavery alone as the later imperial economy's single organising labour principle.

## Inconclusive cells

- **COV2:1800:F Kelantan:** Later nineteenth-century evidence documents debt-bondage/debt-slavery in Kelantan and comparable Malay states, but the bounded packet does not securely establish the same system at exactly 1800.
- **COV2:-500:A Zapotec Civilization:** Monte Albán's early Zapotec state shows warfare, conquest and captive/sacrifice imagery around 500 BCE, but the packet does not establish an enslaved labour status or slavery institution.
- **COV2:1300:B Chimu Empire:** Chimú state organisation used labour taxation/corvée and captive sacrifice, but the bounded packet does not establish slavery as the correct category for those relations around 1300.
- **COV2:1800:A New Netherland:** The sampled target is chronologically invalid: New Netherland is a seventeenth-century Dutch colony and did not exist in 1800.
- **COV2:-500:C Hadhramaut:** Ancient South Arabian epigraphy demonstrates subordinate, captive and enslaved statuses, including later Hadhramitic attestations, but the packet does not securely establish Hadhramaut slavery at c.500 BCE.
- **COV2:1800:D Kingdom of Georgia:** The sampled target is chronologically invalid: the linked medieval Kingdom of Georgia ended centuries before 1800.
- **COV2:500:E Protectorate of the Western Regions:** The sampled target is temporally unsafe: the Western Regions protectorate is a Han/post-Han administrative institution whose accepted chronology does not extend to 500 CE.
- **COV2:500:D Caucasian Albania:** Sources confirm servile/slave categories in the wider ancient Caucasus and much earlier temple slaves in Albania, but the bounded packet does not establish a significant slavery institution in Caucasian Albania at c.500 CE.

The source packets remain intentionally bounded. More research is not performed merely to reduce the inconclusive count.
