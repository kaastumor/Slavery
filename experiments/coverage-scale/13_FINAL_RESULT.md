# COV-002 — Final Result

**Issue:** #151  
**Date:** 2026-09-23  
**Disposition:** **STOP FURTHER AUTOMATIC SCALING / PRESERVE COMPACT COVERAGE REGISTER PATTERN**

## Question

Does coverage-corpus value grow faster than research, review and update burden when the corpus becomes materially larger?

## Short answer

**Not demonstrated strongly enough to justify further automatic scaling.**

The experiment found a real and persistent **reuse advantage**, and a compact register is much smaller than the full COV-001 row.

But the preregistered evidence-maintenance economy gate failed.

Therefore the project must stop rather than convert a promising partial result into a global-ingestion program.

## Expansion

COV-002 added twelve deterministic rank-2 historical targets to the 25 COV-001 rows.

Expansion research:
- 4 bounded-supported;
- 8 researched-inconclusive.

Three of the twelve were structurally unsafe targets before the slavery question:
- New Netherland at 1800;
- medieval Kingdom of Georgia at 1800;
- Western Regions protectorate at 500 CE.

These were not silently replaced.

Combined researched corpus: **37 rows**.

## What scaled successfully

At N=37 the compact register R still materially outperformed the ordinary matrix M on:
- T1 law/practice divergence;
- T2 network/territorial-inference separation;
- T3 direct/adjacent global-handbook coverage.

T4 unresolved research reached parity because M was deliberately allowed a research-status/caveat design.

Thus **3/4** of the COV-001 surviving cross-cell mechanisms remain materially reusable at N=37.

The scale-value threshold passes.

## What compressed successfully

At N=37:

- F total review atoms: **547**
- R total review atoms: **331**
- R/F: **60.5%**
- F median atoms/row: **15**
- R median atoms/row: **9**
- R/F median: **60.0%**

Threshold: <=70%.

**Static compression passes.**

R matches F's practical fixed-task information state on 6/6 tasks, and the expansion-row information-loss audit found no safety-critical omission.

This strongly suggests that if a coverage register is used, the **compact form is preferable to routinely maintaining the full F row**.

## What did not scale economically

Six deterministic maintenance shocks were run.

Results:
- 3 new/contextual specialist sources changed rows;
- 3 searches produced no acceptable new source.

For every material change:
- F touched 2 review atoms;
- R touched 2 review atoms.

The no-change 0/0 cases were treated as neutral rather than cost-saving wins.

Preregistered requirement:
R <=70% of F update surface in >=4/6 rows.

Observed:
**0 qualifying material update rows.**

**Maintenance-economy threshold fails.**

This does not prove R is expensive to maintain. It proves that this experiment did not demonstrate the promised update-cost advantage.

## Full corpus versus compact register

F also fails its sustain gate:
- F does not materially beat R on two independent fixed tasks or maintenance shocks;
- its richer source role/direction metadata is useful for audit, but did not create a demonstrated routine cross-cell value gain.

Therefore the experiment does **not** support continued routine expansion of the full detailed coverage-row form either.

## Controls

All negative controls passed:
- specialist narrative > F/R/M for deep Alodia source interpretation;
- SlaveVoyages > F/R/M for voyage-level quantitative work;
- ordinary M is competitive with F/R for a simple single-cell New Guinea Highlands reading.

## Final interpretation

COV-001 answered:

> Is preassembled global coverage useful?

**Yes, narrowly.**

COV-002 answers:

> Have we shown that systematically scaling and maintaining that corpus is economically justified?

**No.**

The strongest surviving artifact is therefore not a global research program.

It is a **compact coverage-register pattern** that can be used when a concrete comparative task needs it:
- research status;
- bounded proposition;
- required abstention;
- law/practice note;
- network/territorial warning;
- handbook coverage;
- unresolved reason;
- source links;
- access warning.

The richer full evidence rows remain valuable audit/research artifacts where they already exist, but should not be automatically reproduced across world history.

## Project consequence

Return to **IDLE / PRESERVATION**.

Do not:
- begin comprehensive global ingestion;
- start another autonomous scale experiment;
- reopen Atlas product development;
- migrate COV-002 into production;
- claim a complete history of slavery.

A future corpus expansion now requires an **external concrete use case** whose value justifies the research/update burden, rather than an internally generated desire for completeness.
