## Chimu Empire

- Target: `R1:L:COV2:1300:B`

- Research state: `reviewed_classified`
- Classification outcome: `classified`
- Temporal state: `supported_period_level`
- Geometry state: `unresolved_no_geometry`

**Bounded proposition**

Archaeological scholarship on the Chimú polity describes a political economy characterized by state control over land, water and labor resources and, in conquered/administrative territories, tribute and labor service directed toward the capital. Specialist work places a major phase of Chimú expansion at about 1300 and identifies administrative oversight of canal construction and corvée labor. This supports a bounded classification of state labor-service/corvée coercion within the Chimú system at the 1300 anchor.

**Required abstention**

Do not relabel Chimú labor service as chattel slavery, infer permanent personal unfreedom, assume every household or province was subject to identical coercion, or treat debated forced-resettlement models at Cerro la Virgen as established empire-wide fact.

**Evidence locus**

Chimú heartland and administrative/provincial systems on the north coast of Peru, including Moche/Chan Chan and conquered valleys

**Inference extent**

bounded state labor service/corvée and extraction; not slavery status, uniform household coercion, or an empire-wide intensity score

**Temporal display rule**

Corvée/labor-service support spans the archaeological phase containing 1300; do not imply an exact annual event or chattel-slavery status.

**Law / practice note**

The claim is archaeological/political-economic practice, not a reconstructed formal legal code.

**Language / access limitation**

English-language archaeological scholarship and source summaries reviewed. The evidence is primarily material/archaeological and partly model-based; colonial ethnohistoric analogies are treated as supporting context rather than sole proof.

**Temporal display rule**

**Law / practice note**

Royal donation/spoil language records state/temple action but does not by itself define a uniform legal status for every captive.

**Language / access limitation**
