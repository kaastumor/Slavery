# EXP-10 selection adversary

## Attack question

Is this secretly a gap-filling or positive-evidence selection exercise?

## Attack 1 — D-094 explicitly says not to fill empty cells

Correct. The sample is not justified by a requirement to populate sparse matrix cells.

Karnak is selected because ancient chronology was the strongest observed
chronology × frame concentration and an institution target already exists in the frozen
registry. The test is whether the qualification method handles it honestly, including a
HOLD or invalid-frame result.

**Disposition:** survives only as a method-stress selection, not a coverage quota.

## Attack 2 — Two institution targets are redundant

They share a broad frame but sit at very different anchors and institutional histories.
That repetition is deliberate: one success at Karnak would not establish that
`institution_estate` qualification is robust across chronology.

**Disposition:** survives. The pair supplies controlled repetition, not nominal variety.

## Attack 3 — Why not choose four different frame classes?

Maximizing label diversity would make geography/period/frame all vary at once and would
weaken the repeated-frame check. Three broad families across four targets is sufficient
for this bounded qualification tranche.

**Disposition:** survives.

## Attack 4 — Is Sápmi chosen because it may have interesting subject evidence?

No subject evidence was consulted for selection. The reason is frame validity:
`Sápmi / Sámi communities` is a broad retrospective/trans-jurisdictional object whose
historical label and spatial meaning at 1600 require neutral qualification.

**Disposition:** survives.

## Attack 5 — Does a mobile Trans-Saharan network at 1300 duplicate prior network work?

Prior network cases make the method available; they do not make every network identity
valid. This row specifically tests a diffuse multi-route object where a later subject
analysis must not inherit a fake territory.

**Disposition:** survives.

## Final selection disposition

**FREEZE FOUR-TARGET QUALIFICATION SAMPLE.**

The discriminator is not whether all four qualify. A HOLD/reject is evidence that the
qualification gate is functioning.

No subject-research queue is authorized by this freeze.
