# EXP-10 protocol — cross-frame C0 qualification tranche

**Issue:** #274  
**Mode:** execution  
**Subject slavery/coercion research:** prohibited  
**Canonical release:** v0.6.1 unchanged

## Question

Can four untouched C0 targets be qualified or honestly held using only identity,
chronology, frame and spatial-scope evidence while stressing the chronology ×
broad-frame interaction identified by D-094 / DISC-07?

| Target | Anchor | Target ID | Frozen broad frame |

No substitution is allowed after qualification research begins.

## Selection basis

Selection uses only the frozen C0 registry and the already-accepted DISC-07/D-094
target-frame finding.

The sample is **not** a claim that empty/sparse cells must be filled.

It was chosen because:
- Karnak directly tests a non-polity institution frame in ancient chronology, the
  strongest interaction pressure identified by DISC-07;
- Tōdai-ji tests the same institution family at a very different medieval anchor,
  helping distinguish frame robustness from one-period success;
- the Trans-Saharan target tests whether a diffuse, changing network remains a network
  rather than being normalized into one territorial polity;
- Sápmi tests a broad, multi-community / trans-jurisdictional frame at 1600.

## Qualification dimensions

For each target determine only:
1. whether the named historical object/community/network/institution is valid at the
   anchor;
2. temporal precision of that validity;
3. whether the frozen frame class remains defensible;
4. the maximum safe spatial object;
5. mandatory limitations a later subject packet must inherit.

## Allowed outcomes

- `QUALIFIED_C1_READY`
- `HOLD_IDENTITY_OR_TIME`
- `REJECT_FRAME_INVALID`
- `QUALIFICATION_INCONCLUSIVE`

## Source rules

Use neutral specialist/institutional sources for identity, chronology and object/frame.

Do not:
- choose a source because it contains slavery/coercion evidence;
- record subject evidence encountered incidentally;
- use archive density as a readiness signal;
- infer a practice claim from frame validity.

If a neutral source page incidentally contains subject material, use only the
qualification locator and do not carry the subject content into outputs.

## Per-target attacks

### Karnak
- temple complex at the anchor must not become the whole Theban region/state;
- distinguish the Temple of Amun institutional estate from the full Karnak complex
  where sources require it;
- do not infer fixed property boundaries from modern archaeological-site geometry.

### Tōdai-ji
- verify institutional continuity at 800 rather than assuming present-day continuity;
- temple/institution ≠ Nara polity or all temple estates;
- later shōen holdings cannot be projected into 800 without dating.

### Trans-Saharan network
- no single route, city, empire or modern desert polygon may stand for the network;
- confirm a 1300 exchange-network frame rather than a timeless generic trade label;
- route extent may remain schematic/unresolved.

### Sápmi / Sámi communities
- retrospective umbrella labels and changing ethnonyms require explicit handling;
- no single Sámi polity or uniform community system;
- modern Sápmi boundary is not automatically a 1600 historical jurisdiction.
