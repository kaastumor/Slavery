# COV-004 — Final Result

**Issue:** #157  
**Date:** 2026-09-23  
**Formal disposition:** **METHOD SURVIVE**  
**Product interpretation:** **BORING ATLAS SURVIVES; BESPOKE ATLAS RE-ENTRY FAILS**

## Why COV-004 existed

COV-003 left four serious uncertainties:
- whether C1 misses cases that need deeper C2 review;
- whether tiering works outside polity-year targets;
- whether versioned maintenance survives real later scholarship;
- whether the Atlas concept was being abandoned before the stronger evidence model had a fair spatial test.

COV-004 attacked all four.

---

## Arm A — C1/C2 escalation calibration

Masked replay:
- six previously non-escalated C1 rows;
- all three prior C2 rows.

Result:
- material false negatives among non-escalated: **1/6**;
- prior escalations still materially useful: **2/3**.

The false negative was Chandela:
- overall researched-inconclusive state remained safe;
- but its positive 1203 captivity proposition depended on a non-production-grade source.

**Gate: PASS AT BOUNDARY.**

Mandatory repair:

> source-quality failure is an explicit C2/review trigger.

The trigger remains experimental and requires independent/multi-reviewer calibration before large public expansion.

## Arm B — non-polity challenge

Frozen neutral challenge sample:
- 3 node/site targets;
- 3 institution/estate targets;
- 3 mobile/network targets;
- 3 region/community targets.

All 12 received C0 registration; 8 fixed targets received C1 research.

This establishes representability, not a canonical non-polity ontology.

## Arm D — Atlas re-entry

Two actual HTML artifacts were built from identical frozen data:

- `21_ARM_D_BASELINE.html` — strong conventional map + register;
- `22_ARM_D_ATLAS.html` — same data/geometry plus project-specific evidence-layer semantics.

Frozen task results:
- D1 Kilwa locus/extent — no material Atlas gain;
- D2 Silk Road network/territory — no material Atlas gain;
- D3 Samoa fuzzy geometry — no material Atlas gain;
- D4 C0 research coverage — no material Atlas gain;
- D5 Cluny negative control — parity.

Misleading new Atlas inferences: **0**.

Required: >=2/4 material gains.

**Bespoke Atlas re-entry gate: FAIL (0/4).**

### But the map did not fail

The baseline already contains:
- a world map;
- points, routes and fuzzy regions;
- C0 visibility;
- filters;
- evidence summaries;
- citations.

It is, functionally, a **boring thin atlas**.

COV-004 therefore rejects extra bespoke semantic UI, not geographic navigation itself.

# What survives

## Research architecture
**C0 -> C1 -> selective C2**

with:
- explicit source-quality state;
- temporal/spatial inference boundaries;
- source-to-claim dependencies;
- immutable releases and review ledger.

## Presentation
**Conventional map/GIS + tiered register**

with:
- neutral world outline;
- historical/non-polity geometries when defensible;
- points/routes/fuzzy/unresolved geometry;
- visible C0 research coverage;
- no blank = absence.

This is still legitimately a Historical Slavery Atlas.

The difference is that the map is a navigation/inspection surface over the evidence method, not the justification for a software platform.

# Direction boundary

COV-004 is the last architecture-falsification experiment in this sequence.

The project now has enough evidence to choose between:

1. **preservation only**, or
2. **a real, lean systematic coverage programme** built around C0/C1/C2 + a boring atlas/map surface.

If option 2 is authorized, it should be treated as an actual research programme with releases and QC, not another succession of miniature architecture experiments.
