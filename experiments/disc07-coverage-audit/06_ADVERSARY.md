# DISC-07 adversarial review

## Attack 1 — the “blind spot” was visible in the target table already

The raw cohort tables list anchors and frame classes, so a careful reader could infer
the concentration manually.

That does not make it explicit in the current **balance decision**. The accepted gates
test anchor representation and frame diversity separately. A pass on both margins can
mask a failed interaction.

The result therefore survives narrowly as a QA rule: make that interaction explicit
before a future frame freeze if the programme claims cross-chronology structural
coverage.

## Attack 2 — this is just another gap map

Rejected.

The audit contains no historical outcome/prevalence values. It maps target-frame and
research-design state only.

Sparse cells must be described as:
- target-frame sparsity;
- candidate-universe limitation;
- research-state sparsity;
never as historical absence.

## Attack 3 — fill every empty chronology × frame cell

Rejected.

That would turn a QA diagnostic into a quota and could recreate archive-density bias or
force historically artificial targets.

The audit can qualify a claim or motivate a separately frozen selection question. It
does not create target entitlements.

## Attack 4 — sector B is the real new finding

No. R1 already preserved and documented the sector-B gap. Matrix B verifies it but does
not earn a new method on its own.

## Attack 5 — post-R1 research makes the matrix stale

Correct if used as a live research dashboard.

DISC-07 is explicitly an audit of the immutable R1.6 registry/target design. It does not
rewrite later EXP research into R1 state.

If a live overlay becomes useful, it must remain derived and version-aware.

## Attack 6 — sixteen effective frame classes prove taxonomy bloat

Not proven.

Ten effective frame labels occur once, but some are legitimate case-specific frame
limitations. DISC-07 only establishes that `effective_frame_class` is too granular to
serve naively as the sole balance axis.

A compression decision would require a separate first-refusal test.

## Final adversarial disposition

**SURVIVES NARROWLY.**

Adopt one lightweight future-design rule:
> marginal balance checks do not establish joint balance; where broad structural
> coverage is claimed, inspect chronology × broad frame family before research.

No gap-filling quota, visualization, new ontology or registry mutation follows.
