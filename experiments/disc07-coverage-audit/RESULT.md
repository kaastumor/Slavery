# DISC-07 result — cross-dimensional coverage audit

**Disposition:** **SURVIVES NARROWLY**  
**Issue:** #245  
**Historical subject research:** none  
**Canonical release:** v0.6.1 unchanged

The 77-target frozen R1.6 registry was audited with three non-visual matrices.

One material blind spot survived:
- chronology representation and frame diversity pass separately;
- their **interaction is highly concentrated**, especially in early chronology;
- the current marginal pass/fail gates do not explicitly state that limitation.

Future target-frame programmes that claim broad cross-chronology structural coverage
should therefore add a chronology × broad-frame-family QA cross-check before subject
research.

The audit does **not** imply:
- historical absence in sparse cells;
- a requirement to fill every cell;
- target quotas;
- archive-density/prevalence inference;
- a need for visualization or new infrastructure.

Matrix B largely restated the already documented sector gaps. Matrix C showed that
effective frame labels are too granular for naïve balance counting, but did not justify
a taxonomy compression by itself.

EXP-08 remains paused; Qi — 500 BCE remains frozen/unstarted.
