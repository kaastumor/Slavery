# EXP-09 protocol — Grand Duchy of Lithuania, 1300 CE

**Issue:** #272  
**Mode:** execution  
**Target:** `R1:P:1300:D:r1`  
**Anchor:** 1300 CE  
**Frozen frame:** polity  
**Prior subject research:** none  
**Freshness:** target identity/frame are exposed from R1 QA; slavery/coercion subject evidence is not yet inspected in EXP-09.

## Question

What bounded slavery/coercion interpretation, if any, is supportable for the Grand
Duchy of Lithuania at or close to 1300 CE while preserving historical terminology,
target scope, temporal applicability, source dependency and inference limits?

## Existing frame evidence

R1 identity QA validates the Grand Duchy of Lithuania as an existing polity at the
anchor. Territorial extent is historically changing and must remain year-specific.

This qualification is not evidence for slavery/coercion.

## Strongest rival explanations / failure modes

A positive-looking result may be false if it depends on:
1. back-projecting later Lithuanian serfdom or sixteenth-century statutory categories;
2. importing Rus', Polish, Prussian or Teutonic institutions into Lithuanian territory;
3. equating captives, tribute payers, dependants or labor-obligated peasants with slaves;
4. treating later Christian/legal terminology as stable at the pagan 1300 anchor;
5. turning one elite/household event into polity-wide prevalence;
6. counting dependent textual witnesses as independent corroboration.

## Evidence plan

Prefer:
- specialist scholarship on thirteenth-/early-fourteenth-century Lithuanian social
  structure, dependency, warfare and captive status;
- target-specific documentary/narrative evidence close to 1300;
- source-native terminology where recoverable;
- primary sources for bounded events/status language;
- specialist secondary work for category interpretation and continuity.

Search later material only as a controlled comparison; it cannot establish the anchor
without an explicit continuity argument.

## Discriminator

### BOUNDED_SUPPORTED
Requires target-specific evidence plausibly applicable at/near 1300 plus specialist
interpretation sufficient to identify slavery/enslavement or another clearly bounded
coercive status. Exact scope and prevalence may remain limited.

### RESEARCHED_INCONCLUSIVE
Use when relevant broader-period/context evidence exists but target-specific or
anchor-specific evidence is insufficient for a positive bounded claim.

### HOLD_EVIDENCE_ACCESS_OR_SCOPE
Use when a decisive source or historiographic dispute prevents a responsible internal
synthesis.

## Mandatory separations

- slave/slavery vs serf/peasant dependency vs tribute/labor service;
- captive-taking vs demonstrated slave status;
- legal/status recognition vs actual practice;
- Lithuanian territorial practice vs external/neighboring participation;
- evidence locus vs inference extent;
- anchor evidence vs later continuity context.
