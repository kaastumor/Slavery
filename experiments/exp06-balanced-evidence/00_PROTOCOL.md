## Question

What can responsibly be said about slavery, slavery-like status, servile dependency or
coerced labour for six already-QA-ready frozen targets while preserving chronology,
territorial/network scope, source-native categories, source dependency and research
uncertainty?

EXP-06 is evidence growth, not a representation/product experiment. The portable
contract is reused unless a real case exposes a material failure.

## Why this horizon

Alternatives considered before subject research:

1. **Retry Indus/Hadhramaut/Cuzco.** Rejected for now because their exact reopening
   conditions are unchanged; repeating access attempts has low information gain.
2. **Promote new C0 targets.** Useful later, but requires a new identity/QA selection
   boundary before subject research.
3. **Research frozen QA-ready C1 targets not already covered by EXP-04.** Selected:
   highest immediate information gain, real global evidence growth, no target invention
   and no R1 release mutation.

## Special adversarial case: Khmer Empire

EXP-04 supports a bounded near-anchor institutional unfree/slavery claim for the
**Angkor node around 1200**.

EXP-06 must not:
- transfer that node result automatically to the whole Khmer Empire;
- transfer 1186/1191 evidence automatically to 1300;
- turn Angkor's dense epigraphy into polity-wide prevalence.

A Khmer polity result must earn its own temporal and spatial scope.

## Success

Success is six honest bounded research states, not six positive classifications.

Permitted case outcomes:
- `bounded_supported...`;
- `researched_inconclusive`;
- `under_review / unassessed` with a concrete discriminating evidence/access trigger.

## Historical evidence contract

For every case:

- specialist secondary scholarship carries classification, structure, continuity and
  interpretive limits;
- primary/source-native evidence carries bounded events, terminology, law, dates and
  locations;
- exact source/version/locator and independence/dependency are retained;
- missing evidence and corpus silence are not absence;
- law is not practice;
- network/external participation is not territorial practice;
- translated status labels require source-specific category mapping;
- actor identity/nationality is not inferred from names, ports or modern categories;
- archive/source/document counts do not mechanically determine prevalence/intensity;
- no P-level is assigned automatically;
- geometry remains separate from historical evidence.

## Case packet minimum

Each packet records:
- frozen frame and temporal fit;
- bounded synthesis;
- source-native/specialist evidence;
- category/terminology controls;
- law/practice and network/territorial split;
- evidence locus vs inference extent;
- source-dependency audit;
- access/language limitations;
- geometry state;
- adversarial findings;
- exact reopening condition if unresolved.

## Complexity boundary

EXP-06 does not authorize:
- mutation of R1's reviewed/candidate state;
- canonical release promotion;
- schema/ontology/database/API changes;
- a geometry programme;
- frontend/application work;
- a new P0–P4 assignment programme.

The live EXP-04 preview remains maintenance-only. A cheap non-canonical preview refresh
may be considered after EXP-06 closes; it is not an acceptance condition.

## Execution / cost

- Research sequentially in the frozen order.
- Preserve blockers instead of looping unchanged access.
- Batch case work into as few coherent PR/CI cycles as practical.
- Use existing scoped CI; no manual expensive reruns unless a relevant check requires it.
