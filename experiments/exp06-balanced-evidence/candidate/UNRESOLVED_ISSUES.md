# EXP-06 Candidate v1 — Unresolved Issues

These limitations are part of the candidate and are not release defects unless the
human-facing surface hides or contradicts them.

## Chámpa — 500 CE

The political identity/frame is unresolved. Linyi, Xitu/Thu Bồn and later Campā must
not be silently merged into one exact 500 CE territorial object.

## Magadha – Haryanka — 500 BCE

The slavery/status context is supported at the mid-first-millennium / early-Magadhan
level, while exact absolute Haryanka chronology at 500 BCE remains contested.

## Later Mayan City-States — 1300 CE

Mayapán supplies a positive Postclassic locus. Aggregate-wide uniformity across Later
Maya city-states is unresolved.

## Mongol Yam — 1300 CE

Compulsory relay/service obligations are supported across multiple regional branches.
Regional burden uniformity is unresolved and station households are not automatically
slaves.

## Yaghan communities — 1800 CE

Internal Yaghan slavery/servile dependency remains researched-inconclusive. Later
European kidnapping and intergroup capture are contextual/directional evidence, not an
internal-practice classification.

## Khmer Empire — 1300 CE

Household slavery is strongly attested at Angkor/Yasodharapura in 1296–1297. Polity-wide
uniformity, provincial practice and prevalence remain unresolved.

## Review status

All six rows remain internally researched only. No external or independent historical
review is claimed.
