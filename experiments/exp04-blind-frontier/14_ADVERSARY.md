# EXP-04 — Adversarial Review

**Object:** claim that the D-089 extended portable contract survives an ex-ante blind frontier tranche.

## Attack 1 — The sample was still hand-selected
### Finding: LIMIT, NOT FAILURE
The sample was selected from the 15 pre-existing C1-ready targets for structural diversity rather than randomly. Selection occurred before EXP-04 subject-evidence search. The result cannot estimate global frequencies; it can test representation safety across frame classes.

## Attack 2 — Too many inconclusives means the research failed
### Finding: REJECT ATTACK
Indus, Hadhramaut, Cuzco and Andaman deliberately preserve different forms of inconclusiveness. These outcomes demonstrate the method refusing false positives and false negatives.

## Attack 3 — Sumer proves the aggregate should simply be split
### Finding: PARTLY SURVIVES
Subtarget splitting would improve future publication, but the registered aggregate remains a useful test of whether the contract can represent positive subcontexts plus no aggregate uniformity without inventing one polity.

## Attack 4 — Network/territorial note is too free-text
### Finding: SURVIVES FOR NOW
Viking and Andaman show the distinction is essential, but they do not yet show that a richer normalized network ontology is required in the portable release.

## Attack 5 — Category mapping is subjective and therefore useless
### Finding: REJECT ATTACK
The field does not automate truth; it makes interpretive friction visible. Angkor and Hadhramaut show omission would silently promote disputed translations. Indus and Cuzco show not_mapped can be the correct state.

## Attack 6 — Researched-inconclusive is masking shallow research
### Finding: SURVIVES; NEXT HYPOTHESIS IDENTIFIED
This is the strongest attack. Hadhramaut has a relevant target-specific epigraphic corpus that was not exhaustively queried. Indus has a harder evidence ceiling because the script is undeciphered.
The current packet records both limitations, so no historical falsehood is introduced. The project does not yet know whether RI workflow distinguishes recoverable research gaps from hard evidence ceilings well enough for proactive research.

## Attack 7 — The contract keeps growing every experiment
### Finding: SURVIVES
EXP-04 adds no new portable field. D-089's additions are repeatedly used, and the remaining variation fits existing locus/extent, temporal, research-state, law/practice, network/territorial and geometry controls.

## Final disposition

**EXTENDED CORE SURVIVES BLIND FRONTIER TRANCHE.**

The strongest unresolved question is not another representation field. It is whether RI records can be triaged into recoverable research gap, structural/hard evidence ceiling, or target/frame defect.

That is a research-process hypothesis, not yet an ontology change.
