# EXP-04 — Dependency and Claim-Fitness Audit

**Date:** 2026-09-24
**Targets:** 8
**Recorded source relations:** 17

## Result

The D-089 source-relation extension **survives**.

Across the blind frontier tranche, evidence_role and claim_fitness repeatedly prevent a relevant source from being reused for a stronger proposition than it can support.

## High-value examples

### Sumerian City-States
Ur III sources support slavery/coercive labour in specific institutions and cities. They do not justify one uniform state for the aggregate target.

### Indus Valley Civilization
Archaeological sources about inequality, violence or labour organization are relevant context but are not fit to establish slave status. An expert research-limit statement is evidence about the research ceiling, not a negative historical source.

### Hadhramaut
Regional South-Arabian synthesis is strong evidence for slavery/captivity in the wider region but is not target-specific proof for Hadhramaut at 500 BCE. The Raybūn corpus proves a target-specific corpus exists; it does not itself prove slavery.

### Cuzco
State-formation archaeology is excellent evidence for a centralizing polity around 1300 but is unfit for a slavery claim. Later Inca labour scholarship is relevant comparative context but temporally unfit to strengthen the anchor without bridge evidence.

### Viking network
Network sources support slaving/captive exchange and market-node relations. They are not fit to set a territorial-practice state for every connected region.

### Andaman communities
Anderson is decisive for colonial kidnapping/confinement of Andamanese people and specifically unfit to establish an indigenous internal slavery institution.

### Angkor
Personnel inscriptions are strong for attached/dependent labour. Philological debate constrains the mapping of khñum to slave.

### Ethiopian Empire
Regional slavery syntheses support persistent regional institutions. Political-history sources establish fragmented sovereignty and limit, rather than negate, the regional evidence.

## Independence result

The 17 relations were registered under 17 immediate dependency groups because no pair in this tranche was treated as independent confirmation merely because it discussed the same phenomenon.

This count is not an independence score or prevalence metric.

Future deeper packets should collapse dependencies when publications demonstrably derive the material proposition from the same archival item or prior synthesis.

## Decision

Keep source version, locator, evidence role, claim fitness, dependency group and direction.

No additional source-relation field is justified by EXP-04.
