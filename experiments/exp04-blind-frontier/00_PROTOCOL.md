# EXP-04 — blind frontier tranche

Issue: #213. State: ACTIVE / WIP 1. Canonical data: v0.6.1 unchanged.
Selection was preregistered in issue #213 before this session's subject search.
`sample.json` reproduces that selection and pins the original registry. The frozen
R1 candidate registry is not edited when experimental research advances.

Question: does D-089's extended portable evidence contract preserve material
historical distinctions on eight ex-ante selected, previously unresearched targets?

Strongest alternative: ordinary specialist notes + a flat target/source register.
No bespoke interface advantage is assumed. A successful representation may be parity.

The eight cases are Sumerian City-States (2000 BCE), Indus Valley Civilization
(2000 BCE), Hadhramaut (500 BCE), Cuzco (1300 CE), Viking trade network (900 CE),
Andaman communities (1800 CE), Angkor (1200 CE), Ethiopian Empire (1800 CE).
Selection reflects frame/chronology/region diversity, not expected positive evidence.
Execution order is not an outcome quota; Indus is first to attack the absence boundary.

For each: validate identity/time/frame; consult specialist scholarship; recover
source-native evidence where feasible; actively pursue contrary evidence; preserve
source version, locator, source dependency, terms/category mapping, source role and
claim fitness; separate evidence locus from inference extent, law from practice,
network from territory, research state from historical condition and geometry.
Record language/access limits and abstain when necessary. Never count citations as prevalence.

Success: all eight fit without strengthened claims or hidden uncertainty.
Extension: a repeated material distinction cannot be preserved in D-089's fields.
Failure for a class: flat portable representation unavoidably creates false inference.
Do not issue a tranche-wide disposition from one case or from unfinished review.

Outputs: eight packets; shared source relations; extended target table; dependency,
category and access/bias audits; adversarial review; final disposition.
No canonical migration, public release, feature/service work, generic taxonomy engine,
bulk geometry or external participants. In-progress research stays experimental.
