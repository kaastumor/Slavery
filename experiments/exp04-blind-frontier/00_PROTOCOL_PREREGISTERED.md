# EXP-04 — Blind Frontier Research Tranche

**Issue:** #213  
**Preregistered:** 2026-09-24  
**State:** ACTIVE  
**Canonical historical release:** v0.6.1 unchanged  
**Method baseline:** D-089 extended portable evidence contract  
**Selection rule:** frozen before subject-evidence search

## Question

Does the D-089 extended portable evidence contract remain sufficient on a frontier sample drawn from the actual unresearched R1 queue, without selecting cases because evidence is easy, famous, positive or heavily digitized?

## Selection universe

At freeze:
- 15 targets were `planned_c1_unresearched_ready`;
- all pre-existed EXP-04;
- none had EXP-04 subject research before selection.

## Selection rationale

The sample maximizes variation in:
- target frame;
- chronology;
- region/source tradition;
- expected evidence form;
- spatial inference hazards.

It does not maximize expected positive classifications.

## Binding research contract

For every case:
1. validate frozen identity, target frame and temporal anchor first;
2. use specialist scholarship to establish historiographic/category framing;
3. recover bounded primary/source-native evidence where practical;
4. actively search for counterevidence, limiting evidence and classification disputes;
5. preserve source version, locator, dependency group, evidence role and claim fitness;
6. preserve historical terms plus category-mapping status/note;
7. preserve bounded proposition and required abstention;
8. separate evidence locus from inference extent;
9. keep territorial practice, law, network participation, research state and geometry distinct;
10. record access/language limitations;
11. abstain rather than infer absence from weak target-specific evidence;
12. never use source/document counts as prevalence.

## Outputs

- 8 research packets;
- source relation table;
- extended target table;
- dependency audit;
- terminology/category audit;
- access/archive-density bias audit;
- adversarial review;
- result.

## Falsifiers

The D-089 contract requires extension if a repeated material distinction cannot be represented without:
- strengthening a claim;
- hiding a historical category or dispute;
- losing source fitness/dependency;
- manufacturing temporal/spatial scope;
- collapsing network into territorial practice;
- converting weak/missing target evidence into absence.

The flat portable form fails for a class only if a target cannot be represented safely without a fundamentally different release structure.

## Complexity boundary

No canonical schema/data migration, public release, frontend/API feature work, generic ontology engine, bulk geometry programme or external participant work is authorized.

## Canonical effect

None by default. v0.6.1 remains canonical.
