# EXP-04 result — blind frontier tranche

Issue: #213  
Primary gate: method / representation  
Canonical historical release: v0.6.1 unchanged  
Public release effect: none

## Disposition

**SUCCESS — PORTABLE CONTRACT SURVIVES EXP-04.**

Across the eight ex-ante frozen targets, D-089's portable evidence contract preserved
the material historical distinctions encountered without requiring a new field
extension or a stronger historical claim.

This is an **internal method/representation result**. It is not a statement that all
eight historical questions are resolved, independently reviewed, or ready for canonical
publication.

## Historical case states

Five packets reached internally researched bounded outcomes:

- **Sumerian City-States — 2000 BCE:** institutional slavery supported near the
  Ur III→Isin-Larsa transition, with aggregate-polity and category boundaries.
- **Viking trade network — 900 CE:** capture, transport and sale supported within
  parts of the mobile Viking/Rūs network; no territorial Viking-state inference.
- **Andaman Islands communities — 1800 CE:** external British colonial captivity
  supported near the anchor; indigenous internal slavery/servile practice remains
  unassessed.
- **Angkor — 1200 CE:** near-anchor institutional bonded/unfree labor supported with
  specific slavery/property contexts; temple personnel labels/headcounts are not all
  slaves.
- **Ethiopian Empire — 1800 CE:** slavery and slave trading supported in multiple
  regional contexts under fragmented Zemene Mesafint sovereignty; no uniform imperial
  practice claim.

Three packets remain **under_review / unassessed**, not negative:

- **Indus Valley Civilization — 2000 BCE:** target-specific territorial inference
  remains blocked by source/scope limits; direct Laursen–Steinkeller pp.83–84 is an
  exact reopening trigger.
- **Hadhramaut — 500 BCE:** strong regional South Arabian slavery evidence cannot
  substitute for target-specific near-anchor Hadramitic evidence; exact corpus
  recovery triggers are recorded.
- **Cuzco — 1300 CE:** later Inca dependency systems and retrospective colonial
  sources cannot securely anchor slavery/yana institutions to the pre-imperial Cuzco
  node at 1300.

Under-review cases remain part of the completed **method** experiment. The method test
is whether their uncertainty can be represented without false absence; it is not
necessary to fabricate a historical verdict to close the tranche.

## What EXP-04 demonstrated

The same portable structure preserved:

- polity aggregates and political transitions;
- civilization-regions;
- ordinary polities with weak target-specific evidence;
- incipient/local polities with retrospective source traditions;
- mobile networks;
- multiple indigenous communities under external colonial coercion;
- site/node institutions;
- fragmented imperial sovereignty;
- legal context distinct from practice;
- network participation distinct from territorial practice;
- source-native terms with disputed/context-specific modern mappings;
- repeated source chains without false independence;
- near-anchor versus exact temporal evidence;
- blocked access and unfinished research without absence inference;
- unresolved/limited geometry without letting maps strengthen claims.

The most difficult pressure point is Andaman: one frozen target legitimately carries a
supported external-coercion dimension and an unassessed indigenous-internal dimension.
The current contract preserves both through the bounded proposition, abstention,
inference extent and network/territorial fields. That is sufficient for preservation,
though future machine-query requirements could justify a separate experiment.

## What EXP-04 did not demonstrate

EXP-04 does not establish:

- independent historical correctness;
- external researcher usefulness or adoption;
- a canonical successor to v0.6.1;
- P-levels for the experimental targets;
- historical practice polygons;
- prevalence or intensity;
- a need for new database/UI infrastructure;
- public-release readiness.

No source count, document count, personnel count, archive density or map geometry was
used to manufacture historical intensity.

## Contract consequence

**No D-089 extension. No canonical schema migration.**

Retain the portable contract and its explicit research-stage handling for the next
presentation test. Reopen the contract only when a real case/task demonstrates a
material distinction that cannot survive the existing fields without hidden
strengthening or loss.

## Historical reopening triggers

Historical research can resume case-by-case when its recorded discriminating evidence
becomes accessible:

- Indus: direct Laursen–Steinkeller 2017 pp.83–84 or equivalent lawful exact edition;
- Hadhramaut: searchable full target-specific Hadramitic corpora/social-structure
  scholarship/index;
- Cuzco: target-specific c.1200–1350 status/labor evidence or a source-critical
  reconstruction securely anchored to early Cuzco.

These triggers do not reopen EXP-04's method result by themselves. They update the
historical case unless new evidence exposes a representation failure.
