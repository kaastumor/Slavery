The inventory deliberately preserves mixed ceilings:

- R1: candidate/QC state; no canonical publication;
- EXP-04: internal research only;
- EXP-06: published **non-canonical research candidate** only;

No consolidation operation may collapse these into one stronger release state.

## Capacity decision

**Do not admit another subject-research batch yet.**

Reason:
1. 17 post-R1 subject-research rows now exist—approximately the scale of the completed
   19-row R1 internal review programme.
2. Five still carry an explicit under-review state.
3. The 12 researched-internal rows have not passed one common cumulative replay/release
   gate.
4. Cross-batch dependency is already non-zero (Angkor/Khmer source family).
5. Independent review remains absent.

The project has demonstrated enough **internal** review capacity to process a bounded
17-row post-R1 review tranche without new research. That is the highest-value next use
of the accepted method.

## Next mode

**REVIEW / RELEASE**, starting with review rather than publication.

Recommended bounded next item:
- freeze the 17 post-R1 subject-research target IDs;
- perform no new subject search unless a row's existing evidence is internally
  unreconstructible;
- normalize source/version/dependency identities;
- replay the current portable evidence contract and known failure classes;
- preserve the 5 under-review states unless existing evidence already resolves them;
- produce one cumulative **non-canonical review candidate** or HOLD result.

Independent review/publication remains a later and separate gate.
