## 3. Why this sample

The four rows maximize useful contrast without simply maximizing case count:
- Africa, Oceania and East Asia;
- 500 BCE, 500 CE, 1400 and 1700 anchors;
- site, community and polity frames;
- archaeological, source-critical and text-plus-archaeology evidence environments;
- underrepresented non-Atlantic/non-Western contexts.

This is purposeful coverage, not statistical representativeness.

Rows not selected are deferred, not rejected:
- Swahili network: mobile-network separation has already been stressed repeatedly by
  Viking and Mongol Yam cases;
- Ifugao: broad community/local-locus pressure overlaps partially with Andaman,
  Yaghan and the selected Māori row;
- Kokand: valid later polity, retained for a future horizon;
- Lithuania: valid C1 row, retained for a future horizon and not selected merely for
  readiness/convenience.

## 4. Research question

What bounded slavery/coercion interpretations, if any, are supportable at the exact
frozen targets/anchors while preserving:
- identity/frame limitations from EXP-07;
- exact-anchor versus broader-period support;
- historical term/category mapping;
- territorial practice versus external/network participation;
- evidence locus versus inference extent;
- source dependency and counterevidence;
- explicit abstention where evidence is insufficient?

## 5. Evidence contract

For each target preserve:
- bounded proposition;
- required abstention/limitation;
- exact temporal state;
- evidence locus and permitted inference extent;
- historical terms and category-mapping notes where material;
- source/version/locator;
- source evidence role and claim fitness;
- independence/dependency group;
- language/access limitation;
- research state.

Primary sources support bounded events/status terminology/dates/places. Specialist
scholarship interprets classification, continuity, structure and disputes.

## 6. Falsifiers

The tranche fails its discipline if it requires:
- strengthening a claim to fit the frozen frame;
- treating a site/community as a whole polity or homogeneous region;
- projecting later chronology/territory backward;
- treating archive/source quantity as prevalence;
- collapsing coerced labour/captivity/dependency into slavery without source-specific
  justification;
- treating unresolved evidence as absence.

A researched-inconclusive result is valid.
