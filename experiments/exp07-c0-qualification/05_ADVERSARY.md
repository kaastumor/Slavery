# EXP-07 qualification adversary

## Attack question

Did the qualification tranche accidentally convert broad labels, later boundaries or
well-documented loci into falsely precise historical objects?

## Findings

### Great Zimbabwe

**Survives.** The 1400 site/node is directly inside the securely dated occupation and
political-centrality period. The qualification does not authorize a state polygon.

### Swahili maritime trade network

**Survives with network limitation.** Kilwa is strong near-anchor evidence for a
Swahili maritime system, but no one city defines the entire network. Route geometry
must remain schematic or separately evidenced.

### Māori communities in Aotearoa

**Survives only as a retrospective multi-community research container.** The collective
label “Māori” was not the 1700 self-designation of one polity. Hapū/iwi structure means
later subject claims must identify bounded communities/loci or explicitly remain
aggregate/non-uniform.

### Ifugao communities

**Survives with evidence-locus warning.** Old Kiyyangan strongly anchors a Tuwali-
Ifugao community around 1700, while “Ifugao communities” is broader and contains
multiple ethnolinguistic groups. The qualification does not transfer one local history
to the whole region.

### Nobatia

**Survives with temporal approximation.** Fifth-century formation is sufficient to
retain a c.500 polity research frame. Sixth-century state/conversion evidence may not
be treated as exact-anchor evidence.

### Kalabhra Dynasty

**Does not survive to C1. HOLD.** The conventional chronological frame and the unitary
“dynasty/interregnum” model are themselves disputed in source-critical scholarship.
Subject research would risk laundering a contested frame into a false historical
object.

### Qi

**Survives.** Qi is independently recognizable as a major Eastern Zhou polity at the
500 BCE anchor. Later Warring States territorial development remains out of scope.

### Khanate of Kokand

**Survives with early-state extent.** Kokand existed at 1800, but its much larger
1830s footprint must not be projected backward.

## Red-team disposition

**PASS WITH ONE HOLD.**

The method did not need subject evidence to decide qualification. The one materially
contested frame was held rather than rescued. No frozen C0 row was silently mutated in
the registry.

The seven QUALIFIED_C1_READY rows are eligible for a later subject-research selection
gate; qualification alone does not authorize that research.
