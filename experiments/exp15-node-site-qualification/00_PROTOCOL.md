**Mode:** execution / neutral qualification  
**Subject slavery/coercion research:** prohibited

## Question

Can the accepted neutral qualification method preserve identity, chronology, frame and
spatial limits across three untouched `node_site` targets without turning cities into
surrounding polities/territories or selecting by expected slavery evidence?

## Frozen sample

1. Samarkand — 1400 CE — `R1:N:samarkand_1400`
2. Timbuktu — 1500 CE — `R1:N:timbuktu_1500`
3. Tenochtitlan — 1500 CE — `R1:N:tenochtitlan_1500`

No target substitution after qualification begins.

## Qualification dimensions

For each target determine only:
1. identity at the anchor;
2. temporal precision;
3. whether `node_site` remains defensible;
4. maximum safe spatial object;
5. mandatory limitations for future subject research.

## Allowed outcomes

- `QUALIFIED_C1_READY`
- `HOLD_IDENTITY_OR_TIME`
- `REJECT_FRAME_INVALID`
- `QUALIFICATION_INCONCLUSIVE`

## Source discipline

Use neutral specialist/institutional history and archaeology sources.

Do not:
- select a source because it contains slavery/coercion evidence;
- record slavery/coercion material encountered incidentally;
- use archive density or source count as readiness;
- infer historical-practice geometry from modern heritage boundaries.

## Node-site attack rules

### Samarkand
- 1400 target = Timurid-period city/node, not the entire Timurid realm;
- distinguish medieval Timurid city from ancient Afrasiab;
- modern/World Heritage property boundaries are not 1400 practice extent.

### Timbuktu
- 1500 target = city/node, not Songhai territory or the whole trans-Saharan network;
- intellectual/commercial centrality does not define territorial reach;
- later or modern municipal/heritage limits are not historical practice geometry.

### Tenochtitlan
- 1500 target = island capital/city, not the full Triple Alliance/Aztec Empire;
- Tenochtitlan and Tlatelolco require care as adjacent/linked urban units;
- modern Mexico City / Historic Centre boundaries are not 1500 city geometry.
