## Why the frame survives

### Samarkand

UNESCO distinguishes ancient Afrasiab from the medieval Timurid city and places
Samarkand's major Timurid development in the 14th–15th centuries. Iranica states that
Timur chose Samarkand as capital in 1371 and resumed major rebuilding after 1396.

The 1400 anchor is therefore secure, but the node is the **Timurid city**, not the whole
Timurid political territory.

### Timbuktu

UNESCO places the city's economic/cultural apogee in the 15th and 16th centuries.
Neutral chronology also places Timbuktu under Songhai control around the 1500 horizon.

The target is therefore securely a **city/node**. Its wider commercial and scholarly
connections do not convert the node into the whole Songhai state or trans-Saharan
network.

### Tenochtitlan

INAH places the city's foundation in 1325 and identifies it as the Mexica capital.
UNESCO describes the pre-Hispanic island city subsequently destroyed and overlain by
Mexico City. Neutral chronology places a large Tenochtitlan-Tlatelolco urban population
by ca.1490.

The 1500 target is securely the **pre-conquest island city/capital**, not the empire.
