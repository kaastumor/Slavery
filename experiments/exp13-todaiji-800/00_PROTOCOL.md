**Prior subject research:** none

## Question

What bounded slavery/coercion interpretation, if any, is supportable for Tōdai-ji at
or around 800 CE?

## Existing qualification evidence

EXP-10 established only that:
- Tōdai-ji is securely active at the anchor;
- its major Nara-period institutional development belongs to the eighth century;
- the target is the temple/institution, not Nara as a polity and not all later estates.

That qualification does not establish slavery, corvée, tenancy, dependency or estate
labour status.

## Strongest rival explanations / failure modes

A positive-looking result may be false if it depends on:
1. treating every temple worker, estate resident, tenant or dependent as a slave;
2. mapping `nuhi` / 奴婢 or other legal categories without source-specific context;
3. importing generic Nara/Heian Japanese slavery into Tōdai-ji without a target bridge;
4. using state legal codes as proof of actual Tōdai-ji practice;
5. projecting later shōen/estate arrangements backward to 800;
6. treating corvée/service obligations as slave/property status;
7. using temple construction mobilization as evidence of slavery by itself;
8. inferring estate or practice geometry from later property maps;
9. double-counting editions/translations or derivative scholarship from one documentary family.

## Evidence plan

Prioritize:
- specialist scholarship on Tōdai-ji estates, labour, dependants and institutional
  economy in the eighth/early ninth century;
- target-specific documentary sources or translated records from Tōdai-ji/shōsōin
  archives where they identify people/status/transactions;
- specialist work on Japanese `nuhi`, state corvée and dependent categories as
  category controls.

Primary/source-native evidence may establish named people, transactions, obligations,
terminology and dates. Specialist secondary scholarship must carry category mapping,
institutional attribution and continuity interpretation.

## Discriminator

### BOUNDED_SUPPORTED
Requires Tōdai-ji-specific evidence plausibly applicable at/near 800 plus specialist
interpretation supporting a bounded slave/unfree/coercive-labour relationship.
The category need not equal modern chattel slavery, but the basis must be explicit.

### RESEARCHED_INCONCLUSIVE
Use when Tōdai-ji labour/dependency context is clear but target-specific status evidence
or anchor applicability is insufficient.

### HOLD_EVIDENCE_ACCESS_OR_SCOPE
Use when decisive source-language/access or unresolved category disagreement blocks a
responsible synthesis.

## Mandatory separations

- `nuhi` / 奴婢 slave/bonded status vs servant/worker/tenant;
- corvée/service obligation vs slave/property status;
- state legal category vs observed temple practice;
- Tōdai-ji institution vs later/other temple estates;
- construction labour vs estate labour;
- exact/near-anchor evidence vs later shōen continuity;
- evidence locus vs inference extent.

## Qualification inheritance

EXP-10 limitation:
Treat the 800 target as the temple/institution, not Nara as a polity and not all later Tōdai-ji estates. Later shōen, reconstruction-period holdings, or modern World Heritage boundaries require separate dating before use.
