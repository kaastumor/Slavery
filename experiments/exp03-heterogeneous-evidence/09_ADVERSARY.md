# EXP-03 — Adversarial Review

**Date:** 2026-09-24
**Object under attack:** conclusion that EXP-03 requires a small portable-core extension

## Attack 1 — “You only discovered that prose needs to be good”

Could historical terms, source roles and claim fitness simply remain inside bounded proposition / abstention prose?

### Finding: REVISE, then SURVIVES

For human-only Markdown, perhaps.

For replaceable map/table/API adapters, no. An adapter can preserve a high-level classification while still:
- label nobi simply as slavery;
- display a treaty as proof of abolition;
- treat a rhetorical primary text as equivalent to structural synthesis;
- strip the prose warning while retaining a classification label.

The fields are needed because they control transformations between the portable package and multiple views.

## Attack 2 — “Source direction already says supports/challenges”

### Finding: SURVIVES

Direction answers whether a source supports or challenges the bounded claim.

It does not answer what kind of inference the source is fit to carry.

Act V supports the legal claim and is decisive. It is still unfit to establish practice cessation.
The Zanzibar treaty supports an anti-export legal change and is decisive. It is still unfit to prove territorial abolition.

Claim fitness is therefore not redundant with direction.

## Attack 3 — “Joseon dispute is just one weird case”

### Finding: REJECT ATTACK

The same structural problem appears independently in:
- Nahua tlacotli;
- South Asian slavery/service terminology;
- Mamluk/captive/military-slave distinctions;
- Athens' multiple slave-status subtypes.

Joseon is the strongest example, not the only one.

## Attack 4 — “Add a universal taxonomy mapping now”

### Finding: REJECT PROPOSAL

EXP-03 does not justify a large cross-cultural taxonomy engine.

The minimum safe addition is a transparent historical-term + mapping-status/note field.

Anything more would add project complexity faster than evidence warrants.

## Attack 5 — “Mixed geometry means the portable package failed”

### Finding: REJECT ATTACK

The package form did not fail.

EXP-02 itself warned that its geometry hoisting was release-specific.
EXP-03 merely activates that known boundary.

A flat target row can still carry point/polygon/proxy/unresolved state and a geometry warning.

## Attack 6 — “Six hand-picked positive cases bias the result”

### Finding: PARTLY SURVIVES; LIMIT RECORDED

The sample was frozen for structural diversity, not positivity, but it is not a random or systematic global sample.

The experiment therefore supports:
- semantic sufficiency/insufficiency findings;
- source-role and category-mapping requirements.

It does not establish:
- global prevalence;
- economics of scaling;
- external user value;
- completeness of the historical corpus.

## Attack 7 — “Recent 2025–2026 scholarship is too new”

### Finding: LIMIT, NOT FAILURE

Recent specialist work was used where it directly addresses terminology, notably Nahua tlacotli, but the conclusions are not based on recency alone. Exact source role and access date are preserved.

For future canonical publication, high-impact recent reinterpretations should receive normal source review rather than being privileged for novelty.

## Attack 8 — “The six packets are already enough to change the canonical ontology”

### Finding: REJECT PROPOSAL

They are enough to show that the EXP-02 portable release packet is too thin in two places.

They are not enough to justify:
- a canonical schema migration;
- a large new controlled vocabulary;
- a reinterpretation of v0.6.1;
- a public release.

The result belongs first in the experiment/release contract.

## Final adversarial disposition

**CORE REQUIRES EXTENSION — SURVIVES ATTACK.**

The extension is deliberately small:
- historical terminology/category mapping;
- source role/claim fitness;
- active per-target geometry state for mixed releases.

No schema migration or public release follows automatically.
