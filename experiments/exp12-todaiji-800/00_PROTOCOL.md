# EXP-12 protocol — Tōdai-ji, 800 CE

**Issue:** #282  
**Mode:** execution  
**Target:** `R1:N:todaiji_800`  
**Anchor:** 800 CE  
**Frozen frame:** `institution_estate`  
**Qualified spatial object:** Tōdai-ji temple/institution locus; estate/property extent unresolved  
**Prior subject research:** none

## Question

What bounded slavery/coercion interpretation, if any, is supportable for Tōdai-ji at
or around 800 CE?

## Existing qualification evidence

EXP-10 established only that Tōdai-ji was securely active at 800 CE after its eighth-
century foundation and monumental construction.

Qualification did not establish:
- labour status;
- slaveholding;
- coerced service;
- estate extent;
- later shōen continuity.

## Strongest rival explanations / failure modes

A positive-looking result may be false if it depends on:
1. importing generic Nara/Heian Japanese `nuhi` / 奴婢 evidence into Tōdai-ji;
2. equating every temple worker, tenant, dependent or corvée/service obligation with
   slavery;
3. using later Tōdai-ji shōen/property records as if they described 800;
4. treating legal recognition of `nuhi` as proof of Tōdai-ji practice;
5. treating one estate or household owned by the temple as the whole institution;
6. translating all dependent statuses into a single slavery category;
7. inferring practice geometry from modern temple/World Heritage boundaries;
8. double-counting documentary editions/modern studies derived from one archive.

## Evidence plan

Prioritize:
- specialist scholarship on Nara-period Tōdai-ji economy, estates, personnel and
  dependent statuses;
- target-specific eighth-/early-ninth-century documentary records;
- source-native legal/status terminology where recoverable;
- studies of Tōdai-ji-owned people/households/estates that can be dated near 800.

Use general Japanese ritsuryō slavery/status law only for category control unless a
target-specific bridge exists.

Primary/source-native evidence may establish bounded status, ownership, service,
transaction or personnel facts. Specialist scholarship carries interpretation and
category continuity.

## Discriminator

### BOUNDED_SUPPORTED
Requires Tōdai-ji-specific evidence plausibly applicable at/near 800 plus specialist
interpretation supporting a bounded slave/unfree/coercive status or practice.

### RESEARCHED_INCONCLUSIVE
Use when Tōdai-ji institutional/economic context and broader period slavery/dependency
are clear, but target-specific status or anchor applicability is insufficient.

### HOLD_EVIDENCE_ACCESS_OR_SCOPE
Use when decisive source access, translation or historiographic disagreement prevents
responsible synthesis.

## Mandatory separations

- `nuhi` / slave status vs tenant/dependent/service populations;
- legal category vs actual temple practice;
- corvée/compulsory service vs slavery;
- Tōdai-ji institution vs individual estate/household;
- 800 CE vs later Heian/shōen evidence;
- evidence locus vs inference extent;
- modern site geometry vs historical property/practice extent.
