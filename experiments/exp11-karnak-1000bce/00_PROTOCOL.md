# EXP-11 protocol — Temple of Amun at Karnak, 1000 BCE

**Issue:** #276  
**Mode:** execution  
**Target:** `R1:N:karnak_amun_m1000`  
**Anchor:** 1000 BCE  
**Frozen frame:** `institution_estate`  
**Qualified spatial object:** Temple/Precinct of Amun institutional locus; estate/full-complex extent unresolved  
**Prior subject research:** none

## Question

What bounded slavery/coercion interpretation, if any, is supportable for the
Temple/Precinct of Amun at Karnak around 1000 BCE?

## Existing qualification evidence

EXP-10 established only that the Amun temple institution is securely valid around the
anchor. It did not establish labor status, slavery, coercion, estate extent or practice.

## Strongest rival explanations / failure modes

A positive-looking result may be false if it depends on:
1. translating every `bꜣk`, servant, worker, dependent or temple person as slave;
2. importing generic Egyptian temple slavery/labor evidence into Karnak without a
   target-specific bridge;
3. using New Kingdom evidence hundreds of years earlier as if unchanged at 1000 BCE;
4. using Late Period/Ptolemaic evidence as if it applied backward;
5. treating war captives dedicated to a temple as proof of hereditary/property status
   for all temple labor;
6. treating corvée or compulsory state labor as slavery;
7. inferring a temple-estate practice polygon from modern Karnak archaeology;
8. double-counting editions/translations of one inscription or papyrus tradition.

## Evidence plan

Prioritize:
- specialist Egyptological scholarship on labor/status at the Amun temple or Theban
  Amun institution in the late New Kingdom / early Third Intermediate Period;
- source-native texts or securely translated documentary evidence near the 21st
  Dynasty / 1000 BCE horizon;
- institutional/economic studies capable of distinguishing categories of temple
  personnel and dependency.

Use broader Egyptian evidence only as contextual/category control.

Primary/source-native evidence may establish bounded people, transactions, dedication,
service or status language. Specialist scholarship must carry the category mapping and
continuity interpretation.

## Discriminator

### BOUNDED_SUPPORTED
Requires Karnak/Amun-institution-specific evidence plausibly applicable at/near
1000 BCE plus specialist interpretation supporting a bounded coercive/unfree/slavery
status. The category need not equal modern chattel slavery, but its basis must be explicit.

### RESEARCHED_INCONCLUSIVE
Use when the Amun institution and relevant dependency/labor context are clear but
target-specific status evidence or anchor applicability is insufficient.

### HOLD_EVIDENCE_ACCESS_OR_SCOPE
Use when decisive source access or unresolved translation/category disagreement blocks
a responsible synthesis.

## Mandatory separations

- slave/property status vs servant/dependent/worker;
- corvée/compulsory labor vs slavery;
- captive dedication vs later status;
- legal/normative category vs actual practice;
- Temple/Precinct of Amun vs broader Karnak/Thebes/Egypt;
- evidence locus vs inference extent;
- exact/near-anchor evidence vs earlier/later continuity context.
