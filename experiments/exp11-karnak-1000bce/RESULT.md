# EXP-11 result — Temple of Amun at Karnak, 1000 BCE

**Issue:** #276  
**Mode:** execution

**Review status:** researched internal; internal adversarial replay only  
**Canonical release:** v0.6.1 unchanged

## Result

The Temple of Amun at Karnak has strong target-specific evidence for captive/enslaved
labour during the **New Kingdom**.

Around the **1000 BCE** anchor, the reviewed evidence instead shows:
- a powerful continuing Amun institution;
- `bꜣk.w` in a Menkheperre political/banishment context whose slave status is not
  established;
- an Asiatic domestic population within the Great Amun enclosure whose status is
  unspecified;
- Amun-priestly property transactions involving alienable/perpetual services, but not
  a demonstrated Temple-of-Amun slave workforce;
- broader Third Intermediate Period Egyptian slavery, without a target-specific
  Karnak bridge.

Therefore:

> **Temple-of-Amun slavery/unfree labour at or around 1000 BCE remains
> researched-inconclusive.**

## Why this is not absence

The result does not state that slavery or coercion was absent.

It states that the currently reviewed package cannot safely transfer:
- earlier New Kingdom captive labour;
- broad Third Intermediate slavery;
- ambiguous `bꜣk` terminology;
- foreign population;
- high-priestly private/property relations

into a selected-anchor Temple-of-Amun labour classification.

## Method result

The institution-frame test is informative.

Unlike a generic-to-institution projection, the packet contains both earlier
target-specific positive evidence and near-anchor institution-specific material. The
failure is specifically **temporal/status continuity**, not mere lack of source
quantity.

The existing portable evidence contract is sufficient. No new field, status class or
ontology extension is needed.
