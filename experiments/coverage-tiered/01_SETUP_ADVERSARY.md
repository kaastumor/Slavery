# COV-003 — Setup Adversary

**Issue:** #155  
**Historical research started:** no  
**Disposition:** **REVISE AND PROCEED**

## Attack 1 — the experiment can win by defining C0 as almost free

C0 is just a registry. Of course it is cheaper than historical research.

**Disposition: REVISE.**

C0 gets no credit merely for being cheap.

C0 value must come from:
- preserving explicit unresearched state;
- exposing target-frame defects;
- enabling balanced coverage accounting;
- supporting later research routing.

C0 cannot count as a historical evidence product.

## Attack 2 — source reuse can be inflated by attaching generic handbook chapters to every cell

**Disposition: CRITICAL / REVISE.**

A cell-source link counts toward reuse only if the source materially informs one of:
- bounded proposition;
- required abstention;
- handbook-coverage judgment;
- terminology/category boundary;
- target identity/time safety.

Generic regional background does not count.

Every shared-source link must state its material function.

## Attack 3 — batch selection uses broad COV sectors, not coherent historical regions

**Disposition: SURVIVES WITH LIMIT.**

This can make the batch test harder rather than easier.

Do not substitute prettier regional batches after selection.

If a selected sector is internally heterogeneous and shared sources fail, that is evidence against this batching definition—not permission to redraw the sample after seeing slavery evidence.

## Attack 4 — comparing new batch research to old COV-002 rows is historically confounded

**Disposition: SURVIVES BUT LIMIT CLAIM.**

Source-reuse factor is a structural artifact comparison, not a causal estimate of researcher productivity.

Do not claim “35% faster.”

The experiment may conclude only that the source-centric design produces more reusable source-to-cell structure under the selected cohort.

## Attack 5 — C2 selection after C1 is cherry-picking difficult cases

**Disposition: SURVIVES BY DESIGN.**

C2 is explicitly an escalation tier.

The question is not whether random cells need deep packets. It is whether a predefined trigger can concentrate expensive detail where it materially changes the answer.

The escalation hierarchy must be frozen before C1 research.

## Attack 6 — versioned maintenance trivially passes because it defers work

**Disposition: CRITICAL / REVISE.**

The maintenance gate cannot count “mark needs_review” as economic proof.

It tests only:
- whether staleness is localized and explicit;
- whether frozen releases remain auditable;
- whether updates can wait safely.

Do not translate this into lower total lifetime maintenance cost.

## Attack 7 — emergent questions are self-generated and can be post-hoc storytelling

**Disposition: CRITICAL / REVISE.**

Emergent-query findings are exploratory only.

They cannot make a failed core architecture pass.

Require each counted query to:
- use >=8 targets;
- be cross-batch/cross-cohort;
- produce a falsifiable answer;
- alter a concrete methodology/research-priority decision;
- not merely count an existing field.

## Attack 8 — C0 can create a seductive illusion of comprehensive knowledge

**Disposition: CRITICAL / REVISE.**

Every C0 artifact must visibly distinguish:
- target registered;
- identity reviewed or not;
- slavery research performed or not.

No color/score/status word may imply slavery absence.

The public interpretation sentence must be:
> “Registered target; historical slavery/coercion research not yet performed.”

## Attack 9 — selective C2 could discard evidence metadata needed for future audit

**Disposition: SURVIVES WITH PRESERVATION RULE.**

C1 source links must retain enough citation identity to recover the source.

C2 is not the only place provenance exists; it is where richer role/direction/inference metadata live.

If a C1 source cannot be unambiguously recovered later, C1 fails.

## Attack 10 — systematic selection may still be biased by Cliopatria's polity frame

**Disposition: SURVIVES WITH LIMIT.**

Correct.

COV-003 tests a **polity-frame architecture**, not universal human-history completeness.

The four COV-001 non-polity challenge cells remain evidence that non-polity coverage requires a different target frame.

No “all known history” claim may be made from this experiment.

## Attack 11 — 24 C0 / 12 C1 is still too small for final economics

**Disposition: SURVIVES.**

This experiment tests architecture, not final scale.

Positive result keeps the thesis alive and defines a scalable form; it does not authorize comprehensive ingestion.

## Attack 12 — success can become an excuse to resurrect infrastructure

**Disposition: REJECT.**

All artifacts remain flat JSON/CSV/Markdown.

No database, API, map or UI is needed to pass.

## Revised interpretation rule

A positive result may support only:

> “Tiered systematic coverage is a viable research architecture worth further bounded validation.”

It may not support:

> “Build the complete global slavery database now.”

No historical research starts until the deterministic 24-target sample is frozen.
