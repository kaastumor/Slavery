# COV-003 — Result Adversary

**Issue:** #155  
**Input:** frozen 24-target sample; C0 register; C1 rows; source-reuse metrics; C2 escalation; versioned update ledger; bias audit; emergent-query audit  
**Disposition:** **TIERED SURVIVE AFTER SHRINK / STRONG SURVIVE REJECTED**

The experiment does not justify broad ingestion. It does support a narrower tiered architecture, subject to the corrections below.

## A1 — C0: does breadth become pseudo-knowledge?

C0 registers 24 targets, but only 12 receive slavery/coercion research.

Every C0-only row explicitly says:

> Registered target; historical slavery/coercion research not yet performed.

Six identity checks were frozen before research. Two surfaced a mismatch/ambiguity without forcing a slavery inference.

**Adversarial result: C0 survives.**

The surviving value is not historical knowledge. It is:
- explicit research coverage state;
- target-frame QA;
- balanced routing of future work;
- prevention of blank = no-slavery inference.

## A2 — C1: is source reuse real or cosmetic?

COV-002 row-centric baseline:
- 20 cell-source links;
- 20 unique sources;
- reuse factor 1.00.

COV-003 C1:
- 22 material cell-source links;
- 10 unique sources;
- reuse factor 2.20.

Two coherent batches each had shared specialist sources informing 4/4 targets.

However:
- all 12 targets still received target-specific follow-up search;
- the heterogeneous 1300:E batch had no safe shared source;
- one shared source in the ancient-India batch partly contributes a handbook-coverage/terminology judgment rather than target-level historical proof.

Therefore the 2.20x figure demonstrates **source/context reuse**, not a 2.20x research-efficiency gain.

**Strong batch-economy gate remains FAIL.**

## A3 — shared-source reuse creates correlated risk

The early-China batch reused the same source packet across Yan, Zhu, Lu and Cao.

C2 on Zhu found that C1's wording was temporally too loose:
- concrete later-Qin legal evidence must not sound contemporaneous with a -500 target;
- earlier Zhou status terms are less semantically secure than a generic “slavery established” formulation implies.

Because the shared packet informs four rows, the review scope is four rows, not one.

This is not merely a failure. It reveals the required architecture:

> **source packet -> dependent rows -> review propagation**

Without that dependency layer, source-centric batching would make correlated error harder to see.

With it, the frozen release remains auditable and the next-release review can be localized to the four dependent rows.

**Architecture correction required for C1 survival.**

## A4 — source-policy weakness in Chandela

The frozen Chandela C1 row uses a bounded event synthesis whose URL is a general web reference rather than the specialist secondary source standard expected by the project.

The conclusion is already conservative and remains researched-inconclusive, but the event proposition should not be treated as production-grade evidence from that citation.

**Disposition: next-release review required.**

This does not justify silently replacing the source inside the frozen experimental release.

Production C1 must enforce:
- specialist/primary source role validation;
- a source-quality state separate from research outcome.

## A5 — does C1 survive after these attacks?

C1 preserves the demonstrated cross-cell functions:
- research outcome/abstention;
- law/practice distinction;
- network/territorial warning;
- handbook coverage;
- direct source recovery.

But C2 proved that some ambiguous rows need richer temporal/terminological inspection.

Therefore:

> C1 survives only as the **default researched tier**, not as a sufficient maximum-depth representation.

Safety requires:
1. C2 escalation triggers;
2. source dependency tracking;
3. source-quality review state;
4. batch-level review propagation.

## A6 — C2: did selective depth earn its cost?

Three preregistered escalations:
- Zhu — material correction;
- Hanthawaddy — material law/practice sharpening;
- Pañcāla — no material gain.

Result: **2/3 — PASS.**

This is exactly the behavior a tier should have:
- some ambiguous rows earn deeper work;
- some do not;
- deep packets are not universal.

The small sample prevents any broad productivity claim.

## A7 — versioned maintenance: did it really work?

The three deterministic post-freeze update searches all produced no genuinely new accepted source.

Therefore the planned maintenance shock cannot demonstrate:
- lower update cost;
- real positive-update localization;
- long-run sustainability.

Do **not** score this as a maintenance-economy pass.

However C2 created a separate real quality event:
- the early-China shared packet now needs review;
- four dependent rows are identifiable;
- the frozen v1 artifact need not be rewritten silently.

This demonstrates **auditability/localizable staleness**, not economic superiority.

## A8 — systematic selection and bias resistance

Among all 12 C1 targets:
- 0 have direct Cambridge coverage;
- 0 have direct Palgrave coverage;
- 4 nevertheless yield bounded-supported results;
- 8 yield researched abstention.

A direct-handbook inclusion proxy would therefore omit every researched C1 case.

This is a deliberately crude proxy. It does not model public demand or all evidence richness.

But it supports one narrow methodological claim:

> systematic selection reaches difficult targets that evidence-richness selection would preferentially skip.

That is enough for the C0 coverage/bias function, not enough to prove systematic sampling is globally optimal.

## A9 — emergent questions

Three exploratory questions materially change methodology:
1. historiographic coherence predicts source reuse better than coarse sector alone in this sample;
2. source reuse creates correlated review risk;
3. target identity/time validation should precede subject research.

These are not just “more rows” observations.

But they remain non-independent and exploratory.

They satisfy the emergent-query component but cannot overcome the failed strong batch-economy gate.

The architecture solves a different problem than COV-002:
- broad registration does not require deep slavery research;
- researched targets use compact C1 by default;

- source reuse is explicit and auditable;
- review state can propagate through source dependencies;
- immutable releases can preserve what was known at a date.

It does **not** show that comprehensive global ingestion is affordable or justified now.
