# COV-003 — Final Result

**Issue:** #155  
**Date:** 2026-09-23  
**Disposition:** **TIERED SURVIVE — ARCHITECTURE VALIDATED NARROWLY; NO COMPREHENSIVE INGESTION AUTHORIZED**

## Question

Can a tiered, versioned, batch-curated coverage model preserve the demonstrated cross-history value while avoiding the assumption that every historical target needs a full evidence dossier?

## Result in one sentence

**Yes, as an architecture; no, as a proof of cheap global research.**

COV-003 supports a tiered coverage model:

- **C0:** register targets and research/identity state without making a slavery claim;
- **C1:** compact researched coverage for selected targets;
- **C2:** deeper evidence packets only when predefined ambiguity/risk triggers justify them.

It does not pass the stronger batch-economy test and therefore does not justify comprehensive ingestion.

## Frozen sample

Three deterministic batches, 24 new targets:
- `-500:F` — eastern Chinese states;
- `1300:E` — South/Southeast Asian targets;
- `-500:E` — Mahājanapada targets.

Tier assignment:
- 24 C0 registrations;
- 12 C1 researched targets;
- 12 C0-only targets;
- 3 selective C2 escalations.

No C0-only target was promoted after seeing slavery evidence.

## C0 result

C0 succeeds at representing **coverage without pretending knowledge**.

Six preregistered identity/time checks:
- 4 valid at anchor;
- 1 mismatch;
- 1 ambiguous.

The mismatch/ambiguity was represented without substituting another polity and without producing a slavery classification.

C0-only rows explicitly state:

> Registered target; historical slavery/coercion research not yet performed.

**C0: SURVIVES.**

## C1 result

12 researched targets:
- **4 bounded-supported**
- **8 researched-inconclusive**

Shared source structure:
- COV-002 row-centric reuse factor: 1.00;
- COV-003 C1 reuse factor: 2.20;
- 2/3 batches had shared sources materially informing all four C1 cells.

But:
- **12/12** targets still received target-specific follow-up searches;
- the heterogeneous 1300:E batch had no safe shared source.

Therefore:

**source reuse is real, but the STRONG batch-economy gate FAILS.**

C1 still preserves the core coverage functions:
- bounded proposition / abstention;
- law-practice separation;
- network-territorial warning;
- handbook coverage;
- unresolved state;
- direct source recovery.

The result adversary requires two additions for any future use:
- source-quality review state;
- source-to-row dependency / review-propagation metadata.

**C1: SURVIVES WITH CORRECTION.**

## C2 result

Three frozen escalations:
- Zhu — material correction to temporal/category boundary;
- Hanthawaddy — material sharpening of law/practice/status inference;
- Pañcāla — no material gain beyond bibliography/context.

**2/3 material gains — PASS.**

C2 therefore earns selective use but not universal use.

## Versioned/as-of maintenance

Three deterministic post-freeze update searches:
- Cedī — no genuinely new accepted source;
- Kathmandu — no genuinely new anchor-relevant source;
- Malla — no genuinely new accepted source.

This makes the planned positive maintenance shock **inconclusive**.

It is not counted as proof of cheaper maintenance.

A real internal quality event did occur:
- C2 showed that the shared early-China framing needs revision;
- four C1 rows depend on that packet;
- the frozen v1 snapshot remains unchanged;
- the four dependents can be queued for the next release.

This demonstrates:
- auditable immutable snapshots;
- explicit staleness;
- local review propagation.

It does not establish lower lifetime cost.

## Coverage/bias function

Among the 12 C1 targets:
- direct Cambridge coverage: **0**
- direct Palgrave coverage: **0**

Yet:
- 4 bounded-supported findings were recovered from other specialist/legal/inscriptional evidence;
- 8 valuable researched abstentions were preserved.

A direct-handbook/easy-evidence proxy would omit all 12.

The proxy is deliberately crude, so the only accepted inference is:

> systematic selection can preserve difficult and inconclusive targets that an easy-evidence workflow would skip.

## Emergent corpus-level value

Three exploratory questions survived:
1. historically coherent batches reuse sources better than coarse sector-only batches in this sample;
2. source reuse creates correlated review risk and therefore requires dependency tracking;
3. identity/time validation should precede subject research.

These questions change the architecture/methodology and are not simply counts of existing fields.

## What COV-003 disproves

It weakens two previous assumptions:

### “Completeness means deep research everywhere”
No.

A target can be part of systematic coverage at C0 while honestly remaining unresearched.

### “Source reuse automatically makes batch research cheap”
No.

Reuse doubled structurally, but every C1 target still needed follow-up.

## What now survives

The best-supported future model is:

### C0 — broad systematic register
Cheap, explicit, no slavery inference.

### C1 — compact researched coverage
Default evidence tier when subject research is actually performed.

### C2 — selective deep evidence
Triggered by dispute, ambiguity, temporal mismatch, category/law/practice/network risk.

### Immutable releases + review ledger
Do not silently rewrite old conclusions.

### Source dependency tracking
Shared-source corrections propagate to the rows that depend on them.

## What does not survive

COV-003 does not justify:
- researching every registered target now;
- global C1 completion;
- global C2 completion;
- an Atlas UI/platform;
- a production migration;
- automated mass research;
- claiming all human-history coverage from a polity-only frame;
- claiming favorable historian labour economics.

## Strategic conclusion

ADV-001 reopened the systematic-coverage thesis.

COV-003 now supplies a positive but bounded answer:

> **Tiered systematic coverage is a viable research architecture worth preserving and, if explicitly authorized later, validating at a larger and more diverse scale.**

It is no longer necessary to choose between:
- “research every place deeply”; and
- “do not pursue broad coverage.”

Breadth and depth can be separated.

That is the main result.

## Next boundary

No successor is automatically authorized.

If a future validation is authorized, it should not simply add another rank cohort.

The most discriminating next test would need:
- non-polity target frames;
- preregistered historiographic/source-coherent batching;
- independent or multi-reviewer evaluation;
- at least one real positive post-release evidence update;
- stronger source-quality gates;
- explicit source-dependency metadata.
