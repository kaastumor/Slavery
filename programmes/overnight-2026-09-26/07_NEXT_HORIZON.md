## Decision

Freeze one next qualification horizon over three untouched C0 `node_site` targets:

1. `R1:N:samarkand_1400` — Samarkand — 1400 CE;
2. `R1:N:timbuktu_1500` — Timbuktu — 1500 CE;
3. `R1:N:tenochtitlan_1500` — Tenochtitlan — 1500 CE.

All three are already present in the frozen COV-004 neutral non-polity candidate
universe as source-registered/unreviewed C0 rows.

## Why this horizon

The just-completed EXP-10-derived programme stress-tested three recurring broad frame
families:
- institution_estate;
- region_community;
- mobile_network.

A repeated node-site qualification tranche is the clearest remaining structural test
without simply rerunning those mechanisms.

The three-node sample keeps the frame class constant while varying historical/geographic
context. That makes it more informative than choosing one convenient city and less
confounded than selecting three unrelated frame classes.

The selection was made without consulting slavery/coercion subject evidence.

Not used as selection signals:
- expected positive/negative classification;
- source or archive density;
- ease of research;
- a geographic quota;
- a requirement to fill sparse matrix cells.

## Qualification question

Can the neutral C0 qualification method preserve:
- target identity at the anchor;
- temporal precision;
- node/site versus surrounding polity/territory;
- maximum safe spatial object;
- inherited limitations for future subject research

across all three nodes?

## Allowed outcomes

Per target:
- `QUALIFIED_C1_READY`;
- `HOLD_IDENTITY_OR_TIME`;
- `REJECT_FRAME_INVALID`;
- `QUALIFICATION_INCONCLUSIVE`.

## Boundaries

EXP-15 is qualification only.

It does not authorize:
- slavery/coercion subject research;
- P-level assignment;
- practice/legal/network classification;
- historical-practice geometry;
- R1 registry mutation;
- schema/ontology/database/API/frontend work;
- automatic subject-research queue;
- canonical/public release.

A HOLD/reject is a valid result and must not be replaced merely to keep throughput high.
