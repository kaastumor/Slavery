## Frozen research workflow

For each row:

1. start from the frozen target identity, anchor and frame;
2. search specialist scholarship relevant to the target and anchor;
3. seek limiting/counterevidence, not only positive attestations;
4. use primary/source-native evidence mainly for bounded events, law, terminology, dates and locations;
5. do not infer prevalence or continuity from record count;
6. keep target evidence separate from context evidence;
7. keep territorial practice, legal status and external/network participation separate;
8. record evidence locus and inference extent independently;
9. test selected-year applicability explicitly;
10. register exact/recoverable source versions and locators where possible;
11. record source claim-fitness, direction and independence;
12. record language/access limitations and coverage confidence;
13. draft strongest bounded proposition and strongest required abstention;
14. trigger C2 only when Core Contract v1 requires it;
15. adversarially replay every row before finalization.
