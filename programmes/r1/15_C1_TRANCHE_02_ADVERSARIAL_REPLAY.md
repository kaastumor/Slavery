## Why replay is 100%

Core Contract v1 §19 requires release-wide expansion to 100% C1 replay when the first half of replayed C1 exceeds a 10% material-error rate.

R1.4 tranche 01 found four material release-safety corrections in ten replayed rows. The R1.5 protocol therefore activated 100% replay for tranche 02 and conservatively for remaining R1 candidate-C1 work unless a later explicit decision supersedes that interpretation.

This is contract execution, not a new historical classification rule.
