## Why this tranche

The selection is evidence-blind and deliberately heterogeneous:
- all five frozen polity time bands appear;
- new and legacy work are mixed;
- polity, institution, site and network frames appear;
- deep-time and later historical cases appear;
- several rows have known frame limitations, but none was chosen because slavery evidence was expected to be rich.

## Research rule

Every row must either:
- produce a Core-v1-compliant C1 result; or
- remain disputed/inconclusive/held.

No row may be replaced because evidence is sparse, inaccessible, multilingual, contradictory or inconvenient.

Legacy outcomes are not inherited. They are merely prior material to be re-reviewed.

C2 escalation is frozen only **after** all 12 C1 rows are written.
