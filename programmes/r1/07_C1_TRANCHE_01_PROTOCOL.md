## Why these 10

Selection uses neutral R1.3 metadata only.

It deliberately covers:
- new systematic rows;
- legacy promotion rows;
- validated and frame-limited rows;
- polity, node/site, institution, mobile-network and region/community frames;
- deep-time and later historical anchors.

This tranche is a calibration/stress set. It is **not** a representative sample of historical slavery prevalence or of expected release outcomes.

## Research sequence per row

### Phase A — fresh bounded synthesis

For every target:

1. restate the exact target and anchor;
2. respect the R1.3 effective frame and limitation;
3. identify relevant specialist historiography;
4. identify exact primary/source-native evidence where it materially anchors the proposition;
5. record law, practice and network evidence separately;
6. write the strongest bounded proposition;
7. write the strongest required abstention;
8. assign claim-fitness to each decisive source relation;
9. record source independence/dependence;
10. record language/access limitations and coverage confidence;
11. decide whether C1 is safe or a C2 trigger fires.

### Phase B — legacy comparison, only for legacy rows

For legacy-promotion rows, draft the fresh Core Contract v1 synthesis **before** reading/copying the old legacy conclusion.

Then compare against the previous experimental row:
- survives unchanged;
- survives with qualification;
- materially corrected;
- escalates to C2;
- held.

Legacy sources may be reused only after re-evaluating their fitness for the new bounded claim.

## Source ladder

Use sources according to proposition fitness, not prestige.

Preferred order:
1. current/relevant specialist synthesis or dedicated specialist work;
2. specialist article/monograph for the exact target/category;
3. primary/source-native evidence for bounded law/event/terminology/date/location;
4. structured specialist dataset where it directly answers the claim;
5. general reference only for discovery/context, not decisive positive historical claims.

A positive C1 proposition requires at least one decisive relation that is fit for that bounded claim.

Search snippets, general encyclopedia pages and unspecialized summaries are discovery/context only.

## C1 outcome vocabulary

Use canonical post-M1 semantics:
- research_stage;
- classification_outcome;
- bounded proposition;
- required abstention.

Do not introduce a new universal outcome score.

Convenience release summary may say:
- bounded-supported;
- materially-disputed;
- researched-inconclusive;

but it must map back to the canonical fields.

## C2 triggers

Escalate when:
- source quality makes a positive proposition unsafe;
- terminology/category translation is materially unresolved;
- temporal back-projection risk cannot be contained in C1;
- evidence locus/inference extent is materially unresolved;
- law/practice or network/territory separation remains unsafe;
- target/frame limitation materially changes the historical proposition;
- serious historiographical disagreement cannot be represented safely in compact C1.

## Review gate

Before merge:
- adversarially replay all 10 tranche rows;
- this exceeds the R1 minimum 50% C1 replay;
- any repeated material error class triggers a tranche-wide rescan;
- every C2 escalation remains unresolved until its deeper packet is completed.

- no P0–P4 assignment;
- no Atlas feature work;
- no geometry publication;
- no production DB migration;
- no claim that failure/success rates generalize to the other 24 rows.
