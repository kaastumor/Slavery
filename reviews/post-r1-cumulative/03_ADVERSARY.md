**Attack:** EXP-04/06/08 already contained internal adversarial sections, so a cumulative
review could be ceremonial.

tranches:

**Disposition:** survives only with visible holds.

## Attack 3 — inconclusive rows should not be admitted

Rejected.

historical result is still valuable. Their packets positively preserve what is known,
what remains unestablished, and what must not be inferred.

Internal review validates the bounded evidence state, not a positive slavery verdict.

## Attack 4 — Angkor 1200 and Khmer 1300 double-count the same evidence

Partly true if the earlier epigraphic chain were counted as two independent attestations.

The review explicitly records that Lustig & Lustig 2013 /
`angkor-personnel-corpus` is one shared evidence family.

Khmer 1300 nonetheless has independent near-anchor support from Zhou Daguan 1296–1297.
The earlier Angkor corpus is predecessor/context evidence only for that row.

**Disposition:** both rows may be admitted, but cross-row independence is not claimed.

## Attack 5 — Magadha could be narrowed and admitted

The period-level slavery context is well supported, but the frozen target is specifically
the Haryanka/Magadha frame at 500 BCE and its chronology remains the row's own
under-review blocker.

Because this review prohibits fresh source research and the input packet itself preserves
that blocker, promoting it now would convert an unresolved target chronology into a
review-complete row.

**Disposition:** HOLD.

## Attack 7 — Nobatia's sacrificial retainers might not be slaves

status remains unresolved.

**Disposition:** ACCEPT_INTERNAL_REVIEW without slave-status promotion.

## Attack 8 — internal review is being presented as independent validation

Rejected.

- internal review only;
